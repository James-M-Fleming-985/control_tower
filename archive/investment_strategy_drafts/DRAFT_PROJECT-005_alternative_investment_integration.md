## 📋 PROJECT DEFINITION

### **Primary Objective**
**Research, evaluate, and integrate alternative investment opportunities including real estate, private equity, and business investments to diversify beyond traditional markets, enhance portfolio returns, and create additional income streams supporting the £2M portfolio target.**

### **Success Criteria**
```
✅ Alternative Allocation: 20-30% of portfolio in alternative investments
✅ Asset Diversification: Minimum 3 alternative asset classes active
✅ Income Generation: Alternative investments generating monthly income
✅ Risk Management: Alternative investments within risk tolerance
✅ Performance Target: Alternative investments contributing to 12%+ returns
```

### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
```
📊 KPI-1: Alternative Investment Allocation
   ├── Current Baseline: 0% alternative investments
   ├── Target: 20-30% portfolio allocation by 2027-03-12
   ├── Measurement Method: Alternative investment value / total portfolio
   └── Review Frequency: Quarterly

📊 KPI-2: Alternative Asset Class Coverage
   ├── Current Baseline: 0 alternative asset classes
   ├── Target: 3+ alternative asset classes operational
   ├── Measurement Method: Number of distinct alternative asset types
   └── Review Frequency: Semi-annually

📊 KPI-3: Alternative Investment Returns
   ├── Current Baseline: N/A - No alternative investments
   ├── Target: 15%+ annual returns from alternatives
   ├── Measurement Method: Alternative investment performance tracking
   └── Review Frequency: Quarterly

📊 KPI-4: Alternative Income Generation
   ├── Current Baseline: £0/month from alternatives
   ├── Target: £1,500+/month from alternative investments

   └── Review Frequency: Monthly
```
