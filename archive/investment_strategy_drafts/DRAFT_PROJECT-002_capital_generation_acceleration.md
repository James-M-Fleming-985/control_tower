## 📋 PROJECT DEFINITION

### **Primary Objective**
**Establish and optimize multiple revenue streams to generate £39K+/month average capital for investment acceleration, combining salary optimization, business revenue streams, and product monetization to fuel rapid wealth accumulation.**

### **Success Criteria**
```
✅ Capital Target: £39K+/month average capital generation for investments
✅ Revenue Streams: Minimum 4 diversified income sources operational
✅ Optimization: Salary and business revenue maximized through strategic initiatives
✅ Automation: Capital flow systems automated for efficient investment allocation
✅ Scalability: Revenue streams capable of growth beyond target requirements
```

```
📊 KPI-1: Monthly Capital Generation
   ├── Current Baseline: £8K/month available for investment
   ├── Target: £39K+/month average by 2026-06-12
   ├── Measurement Method: Total available capital per month
   └── Review Frequency: Monthly

📊 KPI-2: Revenue Stream Diversification
   ├── Current Baseline: 2 revenue streams (salary, basic investments)
   ├── Target: 4+ diversified revenue streams
   ├── Measurement Method: Number of active revenue streams
   └── Review Frequency: Quarterly

📊 KPI-3: Revenue Stream Growth Rate
   ├── Current Baseline: 3% annual salary increases
   ├── Target: 25%+ combined revenue growth annually
   ├── Measurement Method: Year-over-year revenue growth
   └── Review Frequency: Quarterly

📊 KPI-4: Capital Allocation Efficiency
   ├── Current Baseline: Manual investment allocation
   ├── Target: 95%+ automated capital deployment
   ├── Measurement Method: Percentage of automated vs manual allocation
   └── Review Frequency: Monthly
```
