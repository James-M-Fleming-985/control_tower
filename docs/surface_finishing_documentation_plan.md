# Surface Finishing Documentation Streamlining Project Plan

## 1. Project Overview

**Title:** Streamlining Surface Finishing (SF) Department Documentation for BS EN ISO AS9100 Compliance

**Objective:**

This project focuses on optimizing the Surface Finishing (SF) Department's documentation for operations and training, enhancing usability, audit readiness, and compliance. The primary goal is to develop an overarching document that supplements the SF Quality Management System (QMS) by serving as a signpost for all required documents and forms, structured according to BS EN ISO AS9100 to ensure audit effectiveness and regulatory compliance.

The project aims to streamline the documentation suite by conducting a comprehensive automated gap analysis to determine the current state of documents, identify gaps, and take action to update, combine, or create documentation as needed. The new structure will feature a high-level QMS-type document that references second-tier documents, which in turn reference third-tier documents, creating a clear and organized hierarchy that aligns with AS9100 requirements.

**Scope:**

The scope includes all operational and process documents related to surface finishing and associated processes, including training forms and procedures. Heat treatment, shot peen, and NDT processes are excluded from this phase but may be addressed in future optimization cycles.

**BS EN AS9100 Compliance Framework:**
This project specifically targets compliance with BS EN AS9100 (Quality Management Systems - Requirements for Aviation, Space and Defense Organizations), building upon ISO 9001 foundations with aerospace-specific requirements. Key AS9100 clauses driving this documentation optimization include:
- **Clause 4.4 (Quality Management System):** Establishing documented processes and their sequence/interaction
- **Clause 7.1.6 (Organizational Knowledge):** Maintaining and making available necessary knowledge for process operation
- **Clause 7.5 (Documented Information):** Controlling documents required by the QMS and determining external origin documents
- **Clause 8.1 (Operational Planning and Control):** Documented criteria for processes and acceptance of products/services
- **Clause 8.5.1 (Control of Production):** Availability of documented information describing product characteristics

**Automation-Driven Approach:**
Given light resource allocation including limited manpower and time constraints, the project leverages automation as the primary strategy to maximize efficiency and ensure comprehensive coverage:

1. **Automated Documentation Discovery:** Systematic inventory extraction from CHEOPS/OSR systems using API integration and automated scanning to create a complete "current state" baseline
2. **Intelligent Gap Analysis:** Automated comparison algorithms that cross-reference existing documentation against AS9100 requirements matrix, generating prioritized action lists
3. **Batch Processing Optimization:** Streamlined change management through automated batch updates coordinated with publication management team to minimize operational disruption
4. **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Automated reporting and analytics to identify patterns, redundancies, and optimization opportunities across the documentation landscape
5. **Scalable Process Design:** Building reusable automation frameworks that can be applied to future departments and compliance initiatives

**Project Philosophy - Optimization, Not Perfection:**
**This is explicitly positioned as an optimization project designed to elevate the SF department to the next operational level, not a pursuit of 5-star perfection.** The approach recognizes that:

- **Incremental Improvement Focus:** Moving from current state to "good enough for next level" rather than pursuing theoretical ideal state
- **Pragmatic Resource Management:** Working within existing constraints to achieve maximum practical benefit
- **Foundation for Future Enhancement:** Creating a solid platform that enables subsequent optimization cycles as resources and priorities evolve
- **Risk-Balanced Approach:** Balancing compliance requirements with operational reality to avoid over-engineering solutions
- **Sustainable Implementation:** Ensuring changes are maintainable and don't create future burden on limited resources

**Stakeholder Value Proposition:**
For stakeholders, this project delivers:
- **Audit Readiness:** Clear, AS9100-compliant documentation structure that supports successful external audits
- **Operational Efficiency:** Reduced time spent searching for and validating documentation during daily operations
- **Risk Mitigation:** Systematic gap closure reducing compliance risks and potential non-conformance issues
- **Knowledge Preservation:** Structured approach to capturing and organizing departmental knowledge for continuity
- **Scalable Framework:** Proven methodology that can be replicated across other departments and future optimization initiatives

The REACh team will manage the project and carry out the required actions to achieve the optimized structure within the specified timeframe and resources. Document changes will be processed through batch updates agreed with the publication management team to reduce operational overhead. Critical to success is ensuring that any optimizations and changes to incumbent systems are fully communicated and understood by the team, with clear transition support to maintain operational continuity throughout the implementation.

## 3. Project Phases, Deliverables, and Milestones

### Phase 1: Initiation & Planning (August - September 2025)

- **Activities:**
  - **Project Kick-off & Scope Definition (Week 1-2):**
    - Conduct kick-off meeting to review existing notes and skeleton of overarching document.
    - Define detailed scope, objectives, and success criteria.
    - Establish project governance structure and communication protocols.
  
  - **Stakeholder Engagement & Role Clarification (Week 2-3):**
    - Confirm roles and responsibilities for all team members.
    - Identify and engage additional resources as needed.
    - Establish working relationships with documentation software management department.
  
  - **Platform Assessment & Access Setup (Week 3-4):**
    - Conduct preliminary assessment of current documentation platforms.
    - Establish access permissions and technical requirements for data extraction.
    - Identify potential automation tools and technologies needed.
  
  - **Project Charter Development (Week 4-6):**
    - Draft comprehensive project charter including detailed methodology.
    - Define project timeline, resource allocation, and budget requirements.
    - Establish risk register and mitigation strategies.

- **Deliverables:**
  - Current state documentation hierarchy mapping and structure assessment.
  - Automated gap analysis system with CHEOPS/OSR inventory extraction capability.
  - Validated AS9100 requirements dataset for comparison and compliance verification.
  - Excel spreadsheet report with comprehensive gap analysis and actionable recommendations.
  - Batch change request documentation and approval workflows with publication management.
  - Process documentation for merging, obsolescence, and additions.
  - Stakeholder communication and training plan for system changes.
  - Risk register and mitigation strategies.
  - Initial meeting minutes and action items.

- **Milestone:** Approval of the project charter and scope by the end of September 2025.

### Phase 2: Document Audit & Gap Analysis (October 2025)

- **Activities:**
  - **Platform Documentation Mapping (Week 1):**
    - Identify and document all software platforms currently storing SF department documentation.
    - Map platform access methods, APIs, and export capabilities.
    - Define data extraction formats and scheduling requirements.
  
  - **CHEOPS/OSR System Integration (Week 1):**
    - Establish secure access and data extraction protocols for CHEOPS/OSR systems.
    - Configure automated inventory extraction to capture current documentation state.
    - Map current state documentation hierarchy and structure for baseline assessment.
  
  - **Automated Inventory Extraction (Week 1-2):**
    - Execute automated extraction from CHEOPS/OSR to create comprehensive "what we have" dataset.
    - Validate data quality and completeness of extracted documentation lists.
    - Generate current state hierarchy mapping showing existing document structure and relationships.
  
  - **AS9100 Requirements Extraction & Validation (Week 1-2):**
    - Extract relevant requirements from BS EN ISO AS9100 using digital sources.
    - Validate and clean requirements dataset to form "what we should have" list.
    - Cross-reference with SF department specific requirements and regulatory needs.
  
  - **Automated Gap Analysis System Development (Week 2-3):**
    - Develop scripts/applications to compare existing vs. required documentation.
    - Create automated recommendation engine for gap resolution actions.
    - Generate clear Excel spreadsheet with document IDs, current status, required status, and discrepancies.
    - Include automated action recommendations (merge, remove, update, add).
  
  - **Change Management Process Design (Week 3-4):**
    - Establish procedures for batch change requests with documentation software management department.
    - Define and agree methods for document merging, obsolescence marking, and additions.
    - Create approval workflows and change tracking mechanisms.
    - Document rollback procedures for failed implementations.
  
  - **Gap Analysis Methodology Documentation (Week 4):**
    - Document entire automated approach for repeatability.
    - Create user guides for gap analysis tools and processes.
    - Establish criteria for prioritizing identified gaps and recommended actions.

- **Deliverables:**
  - Platform mapping and access documentation.
  - Automated extraction tools and scheduling configuration.
  - Validated AS9100 requirements dataset.
  - Gap analysis automation system with recommendation engine.
  - Excel spreadsheet report with comprehensive gap analysis and action recommendations.
  - Change management process documentation and approval workflows.
  - Gap analysis methodology and user guide documentation.

- **Milestone:** Completion of automated document audit and gap analysis system by end of October 2025.

### Phase 3: Drafting & Consolidation (November 2025)

- **Activities:**
  - **Overarching Document Development (Week 1-2):**
    - Develop draft overarching document structure aligned with BS EN ISO AS9100.
    - Create signpost framework linking to all required documents and forms.
    - Integrate gap analysis findings into document structure requirements.
  
  - **Document Consolidation Planning (Week 1-2):**
    - Prioritize identified gaps based on criticality and compliance impact.
    - Plan consolidation activities including merging, obsolescence, and additions.
    - Coordinate with documentation software management for batch update scheduling.
  
  - **Change Implementation Preparation (Week 2-3):**
    - Prepare batch change requests using established processes.
    - Create detailed implementation plans for document merging and obsolescence.
    - Develop new document templates and structures as required.
  
  - **Stakeholder Review & Feedback Integration (Week 3-4):**
    - Conduct stakeholder review sessions for draft overarching document.
    - Integrate feedback and revise document structure as needed.
    - Validate proposed changes with SF department subject matter experts.
  
  - **Process Documentation & Training Materials (Week 4):**
    - Document finalized methods for merging, obsolescence, and additions.
    - Create training materials for new documentation structure.
    - Prepare change communication materials for department rollout.

- **Deliverables:**
  - Draft overarching document with signpost framework.
  - Prioritized gap resolution plan with implementation timeline.
  - Batch change request documentation ready for submission.
  - Process documentation for merging, obsolescence, and additions.
  - Stakeholder feedback integration report.
  - Training materials and change communication package.

- **Milestone:** Draft review meeting and approval of consolidation plan by end of November 2025.

### Phase 4: Implementation & Communication (December 2025)

- **Activities:**
  - Finalize the overarching document after incorporating stakeholder feedback.
  - Roll out the new documentation structure with the agreed batch update process through the managing software.
  - Conduct training sessions and workshops to inform the SF department about the changes and how to access the updated documentation.

- **Deliverables:**
  - Finalized overarching documentation and supporting revision plan.
  - Training materials and a communication roadmap for ongoing updates.

- **Milestone:** Implementation of the updated documentation and department announcement by mid-December 2025.

### Phase 5: Final Audit & Closure (End December 2025)

- **Activities:**
  - Conduct a final audit to verify compliance with BS EN ISO AS9100 and the effectiveness of documentation changes.
  - Assess improvements and compile feedback for any residual issues that need resolution.

- **Deliverables:**
  - Post-implementation audit report.
  - Project closure document summarizing outcomes and lessons learned.

- **Milestone:** Final audit completion and project closure by the end of December 2025.
